01 · 关于 VFilm｜About VFilm

面向美国市场的 AI 短剧创作者加速器与 IP 孵化平台An AI short-drama creator accelerator and IP incubation platform for the U.S. market

帮助创作者将原创故事转化为具有商业化潜力的 AI 竖屏连续剧集。Helping creators turn original stories into AI vertical serial dramas with commercial potential.

建立连接创作者、AI 工具、发行平台、品牌和资本的产业生态。Building an ecosystem connecting creators, AI tools, distribution platforms, brands and capital.

与传统影视制作公司不同，VFilm 不以单纯承接制作项目为核心，而是通过创作者孵化、行业活动、技术合作及商业资源对接，推动 AI 原生影视内容产业发展。Unlike a traditional production company, VFilm's focus is not taking on production projects, but advancing the AI-native content industry through creator incubation, industry events, technology partnerships and commercial connections.

03 · 合作价值｜Partnership Value

核心价值不在活动曝光，而在真实生产中的验证The core value is not event exposure — it is validation in real production

把工具放入真实、有规模的短剧生产中，完成验证、反馈与案例沉淀。行业活动是触达渠道，不是合作的主体。Putting your tool into real, at-scale short-drama production to achieve validation, feedback and case studies. Events are a channel, not the substance of the partnership.

Accelerator Capacity加速营产能

进入创作者加速营的真实制作周期，获得持续、可观察的工具使用场景。Enter the real production cycles of the creator accelerator, with ongoing, observable usage of your tool.

Production Validation生产验证

在真实项目中检验生成质量、稳定性、成本与制作效率，而不仅是演示环境。Test generation quality, stability, cost and production efficiency in real projects — not just demos.

Public Case Studies公开案例

形成可对外发布的制作案例与工作流实践，帮助产品建立专业可信度。Publishable production cases and workflow practices that build professional credibility.

User Feedback & Product Requirements用户反馈与产品需求

汇集创作者的实际使用反馈、痛点与需求，反哺产品迭代。Creators' real-world feedback, pain points and needs, feeding back into product iteration.

Channel  Industry Access：通过行业活动触达创作者、制作团队、发行平台及潜在商业客户，作为上述核心目标的入口。Events reach creators, production teams, distributors and potential commercial customers — an entry point for the goals above.

每一场均包含 Panel、展位及品牌专属 BD。季度包 10 场，年包 40 场。Every event includes a Panel, a booth and brand-exclusive BD. 10 events (quarterly) / 40 events (annual).

Keynote

在合作活动中另行赠送。季度包 3 场，年包 12 场。Provided additionally within partnership events. 3 (quarterly) / 12 (annual).

07 · 合作原则 (1/2)｜Principles

独家范围、榜单独立、不作承诺Exclusivity, ranking independence, no guarantees

训练营独家范围Training-camp exclusivity

仅指约定训练营批次以合作伙伴工具为该期指定工具，不构成全年或其他项目中的工具排他。每期仅一个独家工具；全年最多 10 期，名额有限，按签约顺序分配。Exclusivity applies only to the agreed 4-week batch; otherwise non-exclusive. One exclusive tool per batch; at most 10 batches per year, limited slots, allocated in signing order.

榜单独立性Ranking independence

评分、提名及最终排名由独立评审机制决定。任何合作金额均不影响排名；合作仅涉及约定 Banner 位。Scoring, nominations and final rankings are decided by an independent review. No sponsorship amount influences any ranking; the partnership covers the banner placement only.

不承诺结果No guaranteed outcomes

不预先承诺播放量、工具转化率、签约金额或商业收入。No views, tool conversion, contract value or revenue is promised in advance.

创作者知识产权Creator IP

创作者及相关权利人保留其依法享有的作品知识产权。赞助身份不自动产生作品所有权、发行权或收入分成权。IP stays with creators and rights holders. Sponsorship does not create ownership, distribution rights or revenue share.

08 · 合作原则 (2/2)｜Principles

权益范围、数据隐私与交付安排Scope, data privacy and delivery

权益范围Scope of benefits

仅适用于 VFilm 自有或已获授权的合作项目。涉及第三方品牌、平台、场地及活动的内容，须取得相关确认后方可交付。Applies only to VFilm-owned or duly authorized projects. Anything involving third-party brands, platforms, venues or events requires their confirmation before delivery.

数据与隐私Data & privacy

创作者信息、客户资料和产品反馈应遵循合法授权、最小必要及适用隐私规则。Creator information, customer data and product feedback follow lawful authorization, minimum necessity and applicable privacy rules.

交付安排Delivery

正式签约前，以独立的权益清单和执行排期确认交付数量、时间、品牌使用方式、取消或延期处理及付款安排。Before signing, a separate benefits list and schedule will confirm quantities, timing, brand usage, cancellation/postponement handling and payment terms.

09 · 合作愿景｜Vision

让技术创新进入真实创作场景Bringing technology innovation into real creative production

真正成熟的创作者生态，需要的不只是更强大的生成模型，还需要持续的故事开发、制作流程、行业交流和商业化渠道。VFilm 希望与优秀的 AI 视频工具平台建立长期合作关系。A mature creator ecosystem needs more than stronger generative models: ongoing story development, production workflows, industry exchange and commercialization channels. VFilm hopes to build long-term partnerships with excellent AI video tool platforms.

Our goal is not just to help creators use AI tools. It is to help build the next generation of AI-native storytelling studios.
